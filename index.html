B-Splines & Basis Functions Overview

This interactive demo visualizes a B-Spline curve with selectable degree p = 1, 2, or 3 and its corresponding basis functions Ni,p(t).

  • Degree Selection: Switch the curve degree (p = 1 to p = 3) using the top panel header controls.
  • Color-Coded Points: Each control point is styled with the exact color of its corresponding basis function.
  • Basis Functions in Timeline: Graphs of basis functions Ni,p(t) are overlaid directly inside the parameter t timeline. Active curve intervals [tp, tn+1] are highlighted in white.
  • Knot Drag and Drop: Drag knot handles along the timeline to instantly alter knot spacing ti and observe curve changes.
